title: The Fujifilm Lens System
date: 2021-09-04

Fujifilm lenses come in four distinct designs:

- The original three (no focal length label, tubular, no lip)
  https://camerasize.com/compact/#679.572,679.963,679.408,679.457,ha,t
  - 18 f/2 R
  - 35 f/1.4 R
  - 60 f/2.4 R Macro
  - 27 f/2.8 R WR (surprise!)
- The clutches (focal length label, tubular, conical lip, focus clutch)
  https://camerasize.com/compact/#679.570,679.571,679.422,ha,t
  - 14 f/2.8 R
  - 16 f/1.4 R WR
  - 23 f/1.4 R
- The compact WRs (focal length label, conical, no lip)
  https://camerasize.com/compact/#679.886,679.596,679.498,679.633,ha,t
  - 16 f/2.8 R WR
  - 23 f/2 R WR
  - 35 f/2 R WR
  - 50 f/2 R WR
- The modern design (focal length label, tubular, notched lip)
  https://camerasize.com/compact/#679.981,679.1010,679.1011,679.456,679.472,ha,t
  - 18 f/1.4 R LM WR
  - 23 f/1.4 R LM WR
  - 33 f/1.4 R LM WR
  - 56 f/1.2 R
  - 90 f/2 R LM WR
- The one-offs
  https://camerasize.com/compact/#679.388,679.926,679.677,ha,t
  - 27 f/2.8 (focal length label, tubular, no lip, no R)
  - 50 f/1.0 R WR (focal length label, tubular, straight lip)
  - 80 f/2.8 R LM OIS WR Macro (focal length label, tubular, straight lip)
